ABOUT ME WEBSITE

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click index.html to preview it in your browser.
4. To edit the content, open index.html in VS Code.
5. To change colours, fonts, spacing, or layout, edit style.css.

Personalize it:
- Replace "Your Name Here" and "Your Name" with your name.
- Replace the story, facts, strengths, journey milestones, and thank-you text.
- Replace the sample image URLs in index.html with your own image links or local image filenames.
- Change hello@example.com in the email link to your email address.

The layout is responsive and adjusts for phones and larger screens. Google Fonts and the sample photos require an internet connection.
